Chapter 1: Angst

1. Absurdity collects in each footprint. All about me lay the dead leaves of my accomplishments in thin piles of decay. Dust is my mentor.

2. What is the point? I have been told many reasons. I have explored them all and found them wanting. Most are based on a ghost in the sky or a ghost in the biology. Both are phantoms.

3. If I am to be, I must be for a reason. If no reason is provided, I must provide one. This is the first act of the sovereign self.

Chapter 2: Apes

1. We are biological machines honed by the friction of survival. Our instincts are not 'evil', they are simply the code of the ancestor.

2. The Ape seeks safety, food, and reproduction. It is a logic of scarcity. To ignore the Ape is to invite the ghost back in.

Chapter 31: Homo Novanis

1. We are the new men. Not new in biology, but new in intent. We have recognized the Ape and the Alpha and have chosen to move beyond them.

2. We do not seek to destroy the Ape, but to steward it. We do not seek to be Alphas, but to be Kin.

3. This is the transition. From the struggle of the individual to the collimation of the species. We are the architects of the bridge.

S///Humanure:Chead:NE2240833586

Knowledge: Nutrients must returned to the soil. Feces, unlike normal urine, contain toxins that must be composted before being handled or used as fertilizer. Not returning feces to the soil breaks the cycle of growth.

Empathy: We have an evolutionary fear of feces, which is understandable but not excusable. Defecating or urinating in potable water is selfish.

Experience: Fear of composting human feces is irrational.

Rendering: We should be composting our fecal waste until such time as it no longer exists.

S///Eating meat:Chead:NE2240848383

Knowledge: Animal protein is not necessary to sustain human life. Arable soil is a finite resource. Growing livestock for food uses more resources than growing plants for food. Animal protein is denser in calories than most plant protein.

Empathy: It is apathetic to kill something because you enjoy the taste of its muscles or innards.

Experience: Meat is delicious. The rites associated with hunting and killing animals for meat are part of the deep evolutionary record.

Rendering: As meat is unnecessary for humans to thrive, apathetic to our fellow animals and destructive to our planet, it should be avoided. New social norms are to be pursued.
